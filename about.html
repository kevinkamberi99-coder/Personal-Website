About my research

My interest in investment research grew from a broader curiosity about how economies, industries and financial markets interact. I develop independent research to test investment ideas, challenge assumptions and form evidence-based views.

My work draws primarily on institutional and public data, combining quantitative analysis with economic and fundamental reasoning. The objective is not simply to reach a conclusion, but to understand what drives it, what could invalidate it and how the evidence evolves over time.
